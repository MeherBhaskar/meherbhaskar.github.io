I'm **Meher Bhaskar Madiraju**, a Senior Data Scientist at Walmart Global Tech in Bentonville, Arkansas. I build production agentic AI applications at retail scale for real business workflows, plus multi-agent tuning systems and demand forecasting.

I write here about the parts of AI engineering that only show up in production: evaluation, orchestration, benchmarking, cost and latency, and operating agents like real services. Everything is drawn from practitioner experience, with concrete patterns you can steal.

I also publish research on benchmarking AI agents (RigorBench, Benchmark Radar) and speak at conferences on taking agentic AI from demo to production.
