I'm **Meher Bhaskar Madiraju**, a Senior Data Scientist at Walmart Global Tech in Bentonville, Arkansas. I build production agentic AI applications at retail scale for real business workflows: multi-agent systems used across 600+ locations, a multi-agent hyperparameter tuning platform, and geography-based demand forecasting.

## What I work on

Most of my writing comes from one question: what breaks when agents move from demo to production? The answers I keep finding are evaluation, orchestration, benchmarking, cost and latency discipline, and operating agents like real services. Everything here is drawn from practitioner experience, with concrete patterns you can steal.

On the research side, I work on benchmarking AI agents, including RigorBench and Benchmark Radar. I also speak on taking agentic AI from demo to production, most recently at the OMS Analytics Conference at Georgia Tech in October 2026 with the talk "Beyond the Prototype: Engineering Agentic AI for Production".
